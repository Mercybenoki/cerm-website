Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At BenDevsPro, we take the privacy and security of your community data seriously. This policy explains how the Community Events Records Monitor (CERM) app handles and protects your information.

1. Zero Knowledge & Data Minimization

We do not sell, rent, or harvest the personal data of your community members. CERM is built on an offline-first, Zero Knowledge architecture coupled with strong encryption. BenDevsPro does not read or access the identities, phone numbers, pledges, or contribution records of your members.

2. Local & Cloud Data Encryption

Your primary records are stored locally on your device. When you export a local backup or enable Cloud Workspace Sync:

  • Local backup archives (.cerm) are encrypted with your user-defined password.
  • Cloud workspace backups are isolated to your authenticated account and masked in the UI (Session ••••-XXXX) to prevent screenshot leaks.
  • All cloud traffic is protected in transit via TLS / AES-256-GCM encryption.
  • Generated PDF and CSV reports are written to a secure temporary cache directory that is automatically purged prior to each new export.

3. Optional Device Permissions

  • Contacts Picker: Used only when you explicitly tap "Pick from contacts" to pre-fill a member's name, phone number, or email. CERM never uploads your address book in the background.
  • Biometric Unlock: Fingerprint and face recognition are processed strictly by your device's operating system and never leave your device.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase: Used for optional cloud authentication and encrypted workspace synchronization.
  • RevenueCat & Google Play Billing: Used strictly to verify subscription entitlements across Android and Desktop.

5. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your Cloud Account and all associated cloud backups at any time directly inside the app under Backups → Account & Recovery Options → Delete Cloud Account, or by emailing bendevspro@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

Questions about your privacy? Contact the developer at bendevspro@gmail.com.
