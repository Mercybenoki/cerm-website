Official Treasurer Handbook

Master User Guide & Workflows

Step-by-step instructions for every module in CERM. Search any topic below or copy a module to share with your committee on WhatsApp.

🔍
1
Create Your Community / Group:

Open Groups & Clans from the home screen and tap + New Group. Enter the group name (e.g., "Awoja Youth Fellowship") and select its default currency (e.g., UGX, KES, USD).

2
Register Your Members:

Open Members and tap + Add Member. Type details manually or tap Pick from contacts to import names, phone numbers, and emails directly from your phonebook.

3
Schedule an Event / Campaign:

Open Events and tap + New Event. Select the host community, choose whether funds are for "Community Activity" or a specific Beneficiary member, and optionally set a Target Amount, Minimum Pledge, and Deadline.

4
Log Contributions & Share Updates:

Open the Event's Ledger, tap the + button to log Money/Cash or In-Kind items, and use the top-right 3-dot menu to share formatted WhatsApp updates or official PDF reports.

💡 Pro-Tip: Pin your most active groups, events, and members to the top of their lists using "Pin to Top" in any card's 3-dot menu.
1
Independent Group Currencies:

Each community stores its own currency code (e.g., UGX for a local clan, USD for a diaspora chapter). All events and member statements under that group automatically format figures in its currency.

2
Tap-to-Expand Group Overview Cards:

Tap any Community Card header to expand its live summary showing default currency, active/total members, and active/total events. Tap the Members or Events buttons inside the card to jump directly to that group's filtered view.

3
Deactivating vs. Deleting a Group:

When a temporary committee finishes its work, choose Mark as Inactive from the 3-dot menu. This archives the group out of active selectors while preserving 100% of its historical financial records.

💡 Pro-Tip: Tap the 3-dot menu on any community card and select "Copy Group Summary" for an instant WhatsApp status card.
1
Tap Phone Number to Call or Copy:

Expand any member card (or open Member History) and tap their phone number to launch your phone's native dialer (Call Member) or copy the number to the clipboard.

2
Tap Email Address to Send Email or Copy:

Tapping a member's email address opens a prompt to launch your default email client (Send Email) or copy the address.

3
Export Official Member Directory PDF:

Tap the Export PDF button on the Member Directory header to generate an official Navy & Teal PDF roster of either Active Members Only or Include All.

4
Accounting Deletion Lock:

Members who have logged financial contributions cannot be permanently deleted. Mark them as Inactive instead to hide them from new event pending lists while preserving historical totals.

💡 Pro-Tip: Open any member's History screen and tap "Copy Statement" to send them a personal itemized statement of all their pledges and payments.
1
Logging Cash Pledges vs. Full Payments:

Tap + → Money / Cash. If paying in full today, leave "Amount Pledged" blank and enter "Amount Paid Today". If pledging 100,000 and paying 40,000 today, enter both—CERM tracks the 60,000 balance automatically.

2
Logging Structured In-Kind Pledges:

Tap + → In-Kind (Items, Services). Enter exact quantities and item names (e.g., Qty: 30, Item: Plastic Chairs; Qty: 5, Item: Bags of Cement).

3
Adding More In-Kind Items Later:

Whether a member previously gave cash or items, tap their card's 3-dot menu and select Add In-Kind Items (or + Add Item) to attach new items without creating duplicate member cards.

4
Recording Partial Deliveries & Shortfalls:

Tap Record Delivery on an expanded card when items arrive. Enter the quantity delivered today, or mark remaining undeliverable items as Can't deliver (shortfall waiver).

💡 Pro-Tip: Shared reports automatically sum all in-kind items by name across the entire event (e.g., "Chairs: 55/80 received, 25 still expected").
1
Locking the Final Roll Call:

On the Pending tab of an event ledger, tap Start Roll Call. CERM permanently assigns fixed numbers (1 to N) to all currently pending members.

2
Fixed Numbering as Members Pay:

As members on the Roll Call contribute, their name clears from the pending slot in shared WhatsApp reports while their assigned number remains fixed—so nobody else's number ever shifts.

3
Customizing Shared Text, Emojis & Thank-You Spotlight:

Open the Ledger 3-dot menu → Customize Shared Text. Add a greeting, enable the automatic Thank the Latest Contributor spotlight ({name}, {amount}, {event}), customize status emojis, and add Mobile Money/Bank payment instructions in the footer.

1
Official Event Financial PDF:

In the Event Ledger 3-dot menu, tap Export Official PDF and choose either Contributors Only or Include Pending.

2
Beneficiary Handover PDF:

Tap Export Beneficiary PDF to generate a clean handover document for presenting funds and in-kind contributions to the beneficiary family.

3
Periodic Multi-Event Community PDF:

On the Home Screen (Workspace), tap Periodic PDF in the Top Participants panel to generate an executive summary across multiple events in a date range.

1
Local Vault PIN & Biometric Unlock:

Protect your app with a 4-to-6 digit PIN, 2 recovery questions, and optional fingerprint/face unlock.

2
Local Password-Encrypted (.cerm) Backups:

Open Backups → Generate Local Backup to save an AES-encrypted .cerm archive to your phone or PC storage.

3
Cloud Workspace Sync & Two-Way Merge:

Sign in to BenDevsPro Cloud and tap Sync to Cloud Now. If two devices have changes, CERM's Safe Two-Way Merge combines groups, members, events, and contributions without losing records.
