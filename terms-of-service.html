Terms of Service

Last updated: October 2026

By downloading or using the Community Events Records Monitor (CERM) application, these terms automatically apply to you.

1. Subscription Tiers & Billing

CERM operates on a freemium model. Core functionality is provided for free for 1 community (up to 300 members and 1 active event). The Silver, Gold, and Platinum tiers expand capacity and unlock PDF/CSV reporting, local encrypted backups, and Cloud Sync as outlined on our Pricing page.

  • Subscriptions are processed and managed exclusively via the Google Play Store.
  • Cross-platform Desktop access requires an active Platinum subscription.
  • You may manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your Google Play account settings.

2. Financial Disclaimer & Data Responsibility

CERM is strictly a digital ledger and administrative record-keeping utility. This application does not hold, process, collect, transfer, or manage real money, mobile money, cryptocurrency, or any form of financial assets. BenDevsPro is not a bank, fiduciary, payment processor, or financial institution. All monetary and in-kind transactions within your community occur outside of the application in the real world.

While we utilize strong encryption (AES-256-GCM and local database protection) to safeguard your local .cerm archives and synced cloud backups, you are responsible for maintaining the accuracy of your manual ledger entries and keeping your backup passwords safe.

3. App Availability & Updates

We continuously improve CERM's performance and reporting tools. We reserve the right to update features or adjust subscription tiers, and we will always make any pricing crystal clear prior to purchase.

4. Prohibited Uses

You may not attempt to reverse engineer, decompile, extract the source code, or bypass the RevenueCat entitlement verifications built into the application.
